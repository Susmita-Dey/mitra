# 🗺️ Mitra Product Roadmap

This document outlines the future vision for Mitra, broken down into major version milestones.

## 🚀 v1.x: The Solid Foundation (Current)
* **Goal**: A highly performant, bug-free, and delightful desktop companion.
* **Status**: Complete. Mitra runs 24/7 with zero memory leaks, ultra-low CPU footprint, and robust context awareness (meetings, weather, focus mode).

---

## 🧠 v2.0: The "Smart Companion" Update
*Focus: Deepening the illusion of life and OS-level integrations.*

- [ ] **Smart Habit Adaptation (ML)**: Move away from rigid intervals. Mitra learns when you typically take breaks and dynamically suggests schedule adjustments.
- [ ] **Spring Physics Rigging**: Overhaul the procedural animation system to use inertia and spring physics. When dragged, Mitra's ears, tail, and body should bounce naturally.
- [ ] **Expanded OS Integrations**: 
  - **Media Vibe**: Connect to Windows/macOS media APIs to read currently playing music (BPM) and have Mitra subtly bop her head to the beat.
  - **Calendar Sync**: Automatically sync with Google/Outlook calendars to preemptively hide during scheduled meetings without relying solely on process scanning.
- [ ] **Dynamic & Seasonal Props**: Expand the prop system beyond umbrellas. Add sunglasses for sunny days, a tiny pumpkin in October, or a Santa hat in December.

---

## 💎 v3.0: Mitra+ (Premium Subscription Tier)
*Focus: Cloud-powered intelligence, personalization, and cross-device sync.*

- [ ] **Generative Dialogue (LLM Integration)**:
  - Replace hardcoded speech bubbles with a cloud-hosted (or local) LLM. 
  - Mitra generates unique, context-aware dialogue for every interaction.
  - Option to choose Mitra's "Personality Profile" (e.g., Sassy, Professional, Overly Enthusiastic).
- [ ] **Cloud Sync & Cross-Device Memory**:
  - Sync habit trackers, custom reminders, and timeline memories across multiple computers (e.g., Work Desktop + Personal Laptop).
- [ ] **Premium Cosmetic Store**:
  - Exclusive unlockable skins (e.g., Red Panda, Cyberpunk Panda, Fox).
  - Premium props, hats, and custom speech bubble themes.
- [ ] **Advanced Productivity Analytics**:
  - A beautiful web dashboard showing your focus trends, hydration habits, and break efficiency over months/years.
- [ ] **Voice Interactions**:
  - Real-time STT/TTS (Speech-to-Text/Text-to-Speech). You can literally talk to Mitra, and she talks back with a custom synthesized voice.
