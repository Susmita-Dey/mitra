# Mitra Architecture

Mitra is built around living systems instead of application layers.

## Brain

Makes decisions.

The brain observes context and decides whether Mitra should act.

---

## Body

Responsible for animation and rendering.

The body never decides.

It only performs actions.

---

## Behavior

Connects the brain and the body.

Examples:

- Walk
- Wave
- Sleep
- Hold water bottle

---

## System

Communicates with the operating system.

Examples:

- Idle detection
- Battery
- Time
- Startup
- Window management

---

## Storage

Stores user preferences and reminder history locally.

No cloud.

No accounts.

No tracking.
