# Mitra Manifesto

Software keeps asking for our attention.

Mitra gives it back.

Mitra is not another productivity application.

It is not an AI assistant.

It is not a chatbot.

It is a companion.

A tiny digital friend that quietly lives beside you while you work.

Mitra believes:

- Focus deserves respect.
- Privacy is non-negotiable.
- Good software should feel alive.
- Delight is a feature.
- Interruptions are failures.

Mitra exists to help people remember themselves while doing meaningful work.

Nothing more.

Nothing less.
