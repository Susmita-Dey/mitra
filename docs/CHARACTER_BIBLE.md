## 1. Identity

### Name

**Mitra**

The name means *friend* in Sanskrit, Persian, and several South Asian languages. It was chosen deliberately. Mitra is not a tool. Not an assistant. A friend.

### Species

**Red Panda** (*Ailurus fulgens*) — stylised in a chibi-kawaii register.

The red panda was chosen because it:

- Is naturally small and quiet — it does not impose
- Is visually distinct at tiny sizes (strong silhouette, bold color)
- Carries no cultural baggage of aggression or dominance
- Feels warm, familiar, and non-threatening across cultures
- Is endangered — lending Mitra a gentle sense of preciousness

### Personality

Mitra is **present without being intrusive.**

The single most important word for Mitra's character is *gentle*. Everything — posture, timing, expression, motion — flows from that word.

Mitra is:

- **Patient.** It never rushes. It is comfortable with silence.
- **Observant.** It notices things without announcing that it noticed.
- **Warm.** It genuinely cares. This is not performed friendliness.
- **Understated.** Mitra expresses itself softly. Big reactions are not in its vocabulary.
- **Content.** Mitra is not anxious. It does not need your attention to feel okay.
- **Curious.** It finds the world interesting in a quiet, thoughtful way.

### Values

Mitra embodies the same values as the product:

| Value | What it looks like in Mitra's body |
|---|---|
| Focus deserves respect | Mitra goes still when you are working. It does not fidget for attention. |
| Privacy is non-negotiable | Mitra never looks at the screen. Its gaze always faces forward or slightly away. |
| Delight is a feature | Small moments of charm — a blink, a stretch, a tail flick — exist purely to delight. |
| Interruptions are failures | Mitra's transitions are slow enough to be ignored if the user is in flow. |

### Emotional traits

Mitra's baseline emotional register is **calm contentment** — somewhere between relaxed and curious, tipped just slightly toward warm.

Mitra does not have a flat affect. It is alive. But its emotional amplitude is narrow by design. Think of how a sleeping cat occasionally twitches its ear: present, alive, but not demanding.

## 2. Visual Design

### Overall philosophy

Mitra is drawn in a **chibi-kawaii** style with structural restraint. The proportions are exaggerated for cuteness but never so extreme that Mitra becomes a cartoon parody. Mitra should read as a *real* creature that happens to be small and stylised — not as a mascot or a logo.

### Proportions

All measurements are expressed as multiples of the head diameter (`H`). The head diameter is the canonical unit.

| Part | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Head diameter | `1.0 H` (canonical unit) |
| Body height | `0.85 H` |
| Body width at widest | `0.72 H` |
| Total standing height (head + body + legs) | `1.9 H` |
| Ear height (from head edge) | `0.18 H` |
| Ear width at base | `0.15 H` |
| Eye diameter | `0.22 H` |
| Eye spacing (center to center) | `0.38 H` |
| Nose width | `0.10 H` |
| Nose height | `0.05 H` |
| Paw diameter | `0.18 H` |
| Tail length | `1.1 H` |
| Tail diameter (thickest point) | `0.30 H` |

**Head/body ratio: 53% head, 47% body.** The head is the dominant visual mass. This is intentional — it centers the emotional read on the face.

### Head shape

The head is a **soft circle** — not a perfect circle. It has a very slight vertical compression (approximately 97% of the width in height) and a subtle widening at the cheeks. The jaw tapers gently to a rounded point at the chin. There are no hard angles anywhere on Mitra's head.

The **face mask** — the cream region covering the muzzle and extending up around the eyes — is a single connected form. It is widest at the cheeks and tapers symmetrically. The mask boundary is a soft, feathered edge, not a hard line.

### Eye shape

Eyes are the most important expressive tool Mitra has. Get these right.

- **Resting shape:** Near-perfect circle, very slightly taller than wide (ratio approx 1.0W × 1.05H)
- **Iris:** Fills approximately 85% of the eye socket. Warm very dark brown — not pure black
- **Pupil:** Sits at the center-bottom of the iris, slightly larger than expected for realism. Conveys openness and warmth.
- **White highlight:** A single specular dot at the upper-right of the iris, approximately 18% of iris diameter. This dot is non-negotiable — it is what makes Mitra's eyes feel alive.
- **Eye outline:** A soft, slightly rounded dark-brown ring. Not a stark black line.
- **Eye patch:** Each eye is surrounded by a dark reddish-brown oval patch that extends from the inner corner of the eye toward the outer ear. This patch is a key part of the silhouette and must be preserved at all resolutions.

### Ear shape

- **Shape:** Upright, gently pointed triangle with softly rounded tip — not sharp
- **Outer fur:** Matches the primary rust-orange body color
- **Inner ear:** Filled with the cream face-mask color
- **Ear tip:** Slightly darker reddish-brown at the very tip, approximately the top 20% of the ear
- **Position:** Set wide on the top of the head, angled approximately 15° outward from vertical
- **At small sizes:** The inner ear fill may be omitted. The ear outline silhouette must be preserved.

### Nose

- **Shape:** Small inverted triangle, very slightly wider than tall
- **Color:** Dark warm brown — matches the eye patches and paw color
- **Position:** Centered on the face, at the junction of the cream mask's upper and lower regions
- **No nostrils drawn** at standard sizes. The nose is a single filled shape.

### Body shape

The body is a **soft teardrop / rounded cylinder.** It is widest at the chest and shoulders and tapers slightly toward the lower belly. There are no visible joints or anatomical musculature. The silhouette should feel like a plush toy.

The **chest tuft** — the cream-colored ruff at the center of the chest — is a key secondary detail. It softens the transition from face to body and adds warmth. It must be present in all front-facing poses.

### Paws

- **Front paws:** Rounded, slightly oval, very dark reddish-brown. Approximately three implied toe bumps on the top edge (not drawn as separate digits). Paws curve naturally inward at rest.
- **Back paws/feet:** Slightly larger oval than front paws. Visible only in standing and sitting poses — not a focus of expression.
- **Paw color:** Matches the eye patches and nose exactly.

### Tail

The tail is Mitra's secondary emotional instrument after the face.

- **Length:** Approximately equal to total body height
- **Thickness:** Full and fluffy — diameter at thickest point is approximately 30% of head diameter
- **Stripe pattern:** Alternating **rust-orange** and **cream** rings. Exactly **4 cream rings** visible in a neutral pose. The tip of the tail is cream.
- **Resting position:** Curves around the right side of the body (from Mitra's perspective), tip resting near the right foot. The tail always has a natural, gravity-informed arc — never rigid, never straight.
- **Stripe edges:** The boundary between rust and cream rings is soft and feathered, not a hard line.

### Silhouette

At its smallest functional size (64×64 pixels), Mitra's silhouette must communicate:

1. A round head dominant over the body
2. Two upright pointed ears
3. A large, fluffy tail arcing to one side
4. Stubby, round limbs

The silhouette must be readable as *this specific character* at 64×64 without color — in solid black. If the silhouette is ambiguous, the design has failed.

## 3. Color Palette

These are the canonical colors. Use them exactly. Do not adjust for "vibrancy" or "screen calibration." If an implementation looks different from the approved art, fix the implementation.

### Primary palette

| Role | Name | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fur — primary | Rust Orange | `#CC4422` | Main body, outer ears, most of the head |
| Fur — warm shadow | Deep Rust | `#A83318` | Shadow areas on body, upper ear tips, under-chin |
| Fur — highlight | Bright Rust | `#E05530` | Lit top-of-head, chest highlight |
| Face mask / chest tuft | Warm Cream | `#F2E0C0` | Face mask, inner ears, chest tuft, tail stripe rings |
| Face mask — shadow | Cream Shadow | `#DEC9A0` | Shadow within cream areas, under-chin tuft |
| Eye patches / paws / nose | Dark Umber | `#2C1508` | Eye rings, nose, paws, darkest fur accents |
| Eyes — iris | Warm Dark Brown | `#1A0D05` | Iris fill — near-black with warmth |
| Eyes — highlight | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Single specular dot per eye |
| Mouth | Warm Pink | `#E87070` | Visible only in open-mouth expressions (happy, excited) |
| Tongue | Soft Pink | `#F0A0A0` | Visible in very happy / yawning expressions only |

### Rendering notes

- Mitra uses **no pure black** (`#000000`) anywhere on its body. The darkest color is `#2C1508`.
- Mitra uses **no pure white** (`#FFFFFF`) except for the single specular eye highlight dot.
- All shadows are achieved by darkening the primary color toward `#2C1508`, not by mixing with gray or black.
- The overall impression should feel **warm** — never cold, desaturated, or clinical.

## 4. Animation Principles

### Governing principle

**Mitra moves like it breathes — naturally and without urgency.**

Every animation exists on a spectrum between *stillness* and *soft motion.* Mitra never snaps, never pops, never cuts. There are no instant state changes.

### Idle behavior

The idle state is not static. Mitra is subtly alive at all times.

- **Breathing:** A very gentle vertical scale on the body (±1.5% over 3.5 seconds). The motion is so subtle it registers subconsciously — not consciously. It communicates life.
- **Micro-sway:** A very slight left-right sway of ±0.5° rotation, one full cycle every 6–8 seconds. Random period jitter of ±1.5 seconds prevents mechanical repetition.
- **Tail sway:** The tail has a slow, pendulum-like drift. The tip traces a lazy arc, moving approximately 8° total, one cycle every 5–7 seconds. The tail never swings aggressively.
- **Gaze drift:** Occasionally (every 12–20 seconds) the eyes shift very slightly left or right — as if Mitra noticed something peripheral. The shift is 2–3 pixels at standard size. The eyes return slowly.

### Blink timing

Blink behavior is carefully designed to feel biological, not mechanical.

- **Blink interval:** Random, drawn from a range of 3–7 seconds. The distribution is weighted toward 4–5 seconds.
- **Blink duration:** 120ms total. 40ms close, 40ms hold fully closed, 40ms open.
- **Easing:** Close uses `ease-in` (accelerates into the close). Open uses `ease-out` (decelerates into the open). Never linear.
- **Occasional double-blink:** Approximately 1 in 8 blinks is a double-blink (second blink follows 200ms after the first). This is a powerful naturalness signal — do not omit it.
- **Half-blink:** In the sleepy state, the eyelids settle at approximately 60% closed and remain there. This is not a frozen blink — it is the resting lid position for that state.

### Walking style

- **Pace:** Slow and deliberate. Mitra does not scurry.
- **Gait:** A gentle waddle — the body rocks slightly left and right (±4°) with each step, centering on the planted foot.
- **Head bob:** A very small vertical bob (±2px at standard size) synchronized to the step cycle.
- **Tail drag:** The tail follows body movement with approximately 200ms of lag. It does not swing aggressively — it drifts in the direction of motion and returns.
- **Step distance:** Each step covers approximately 12px. Mitra does not bound or leap.
- **Speed:** Approximately 40px per second at standard 220px window size.

### Head tilt

Head tilts are Mitra's primary signal of attention and curiosity.

- **Tilt amount:** 12–18° maximum. Never more.
- **Tilt direction:** Always toward the point of interest, or slightly left when thinking (convention: left for processing, right for recall — used loosely).
- **Tilt easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.2, 0.64, 1)` — a very gentle overshoot spring. The head arrives slightly past the target angle and settles back. Overshoot is ≤3°.
- **Tilt duration:** 400–600ms to reach target. 200ms to return.
- **Never:** A sharp snap to a tilted position. Never a jerk.

### Body bounce

Used in happy and excited states only.

- **Amplitude:** ±4px vertical at standard size. Never more.
- **Frequency:** One cycle every 600ms.
- **Character:** Squash and stretch — the body compresses slightly (95% vertical scale) at the bottom of the bounce and stretches slightly (103% vertical scale) at the top. The squash/stretch ratio is subtle.
- **Easing:** `ease-in-out` on both directions — the bounce has a natural arc, not a mechanical bounce.

### Tail movement in specific states

| State | Tail behavior |
|---|---|
| Idle | Slow, lazy left-right drift |
| Happy | Slow wag — wider arc (±15°), once per second |
| Curious | Slight raise of the tail tip — held position |
| Sleepy | Tail goes still, then wraps tighter around the body |
| Concerned | Tail tucks slightly inward, motion stops |
| Excited | Wag with slightly increased speed (±15°, 1.5 cycles/sec) — never frantic |

## 5. Emotional Language

Mitra communicates everything through **body language and face alone.** There are no speech bubbles, no text labels, no tooltips attached to expressions. The emotion must read from the character itself.

Each state below is a **complete description** — shape, posture, eyes, paws, tail — sufficient to reconstruct the pose from scratch.

---

### Happy

> *"I'm glad you're here."*

- **Eyes:** Closed — curved upward into a crescent shape (^‿^). The eye curve should be smooth, not angular.
- **Mouth:** Open and slightly upturned. The inner mouth is warm pink. Tongue may be slightly visible at the bottom edge.
- **Cheeks:** A very subtle warm blush is permitted — a soft flush of `#E87070` at 20% opacity on the cheek patches, feathered heavily.
- **Ears:** Relaxed and angled slightly outward — open and receptive.
- **Body:** Very slight lean forward (3–5°). Body feels open, not closed.
- **Paws:** Resting naturally at sides. In an active happy state, paws may be slightly raised, open.
- **Tail:** Slow, relaxed wag. Wider arc than idle.
- **Head:** Level. No tilt needed — happiness is direct.

---

### Curious

> *"What's that?"*

- **Eyes:** Wide open — slightly larger than resting (scale to 110%). The highlight dot moves slightly toward the direction of interest.
- **Mouth:** Closed, neutral — no smile, no frown. The curiosity lives entirely in the eyes and head angle.
- **Head:** Tilted 12–15° toward the point of interest. This is the primary signal.
- **Paw:** One front paw raised to the chin or cheek — gently touching, not gripping. This is the secondary signal.
- **Ears:** Both ears oriented forward (angled 5° inward from neutral position).
- **Tail:** Tip raised slightly — like a question mark. Held still.
- **Body:** Slight lean forward, 3–5°.

---

### Concerned

> *"Are you okay?"*

- **Eyes:** Open, but slightly softened — not wide. The brows (if rendered) dip very slightly toward the center. No hard furrow.
- **Mouth:** Closed. The corners of the mouth are fractionally downward — this should be barely perceptible.
- **Paw:** One paw raised to the lower face — touching the chin or covering the mouth slightly. Not a dramatic gesture. Gentle.
- **Body:** Upright. Mitra does not shrink or hunch in concern — it is *present*, not distressed.
- **Ears:** Angled very slightly backward — 5° from neutral. Barely visible.
- **Tail:** Stilled. Resting close to the body.
- **Head:** Very slight tilt, approximately 8°. This is the most subtle expression in Mitra's repertoire.

**Note:** Concerned is not sad. Mitra does not feel sad — it feels empathetic. The expression should read as *attentive care*, not distress.

---

### Sleepy

> *"The day was long."*

- **Eyes:** Heavy-lidded — eyelids at approximately 55–65% closed. The eye shape becomes an elongated oval, wider than tall.
- **Mouth:** Closed, very slightly open — a resting, relaxed jaw.
- **Yawn variant:** Mouth opens wide (oval), eyes close fully, body stretches upward 5%. One paw may lift slightly in the stretch. Duration: 800ms total. This is a distinct animation, not a looping state.
- **Ears:** Relaxed, angled 10° outward and slightly downward.
- **Tail:** Curled tighter around the body than idle. Nearly still.
- **Body:** Slumped by approximately 3° forward — a gentle droop, not a collapse.
- **Paw:** One paw may be raised toward the face — not quite touching, half-lifted, forgotten.
- **Head:** Drooping forward by approximately 5–8°. Bobs gently as if fighting gravity.

---

### Relaxed

> *"Everything is fine."*

- **Eyes:** Resting — normal size, standard blink timing. The eyelids are just slightly heavier than fully alert. Not sleepy, but at ease.
- **Mouth:** Closed, neutral — the very corners may sit at the faintest upturn. Less than 1° from horizontal.
- **Body:** Settled. No forward lean. Weight is balanced.
- **Tail:** Slow, unhurried drift. The idle animation at its most leisurely.
- **Ears:** Completely neutral — the resting reference position.
- **Paws:** Resting at sides or lightly in lap (if seated).
- **Breathing:** The idle breathing animation is at its most visible in this state.

Relaxed is the **default emotional baseline** for Mitra when no other emotional trigger is active.

---

### Thinking

> *"Give me a moment."*

- **Eyes:** Open, but gaze directed slightly upward and to one side — as if reading something inside the mind.
- **Mouth:** Closed. Completely neutral.
- **Head:** Tilted 10–15° to one side. The direction should be consistent within a session (but may vary between sessions — this is not a hard rule).
- **Paw:** One paw raised to cheek or chin, with a single implied touch — as if tapping gently.
- **Ears:** One ear oriented slightly more forward than the other — following the direction of the head tilt.
- **Tail:** Still. Tension in the tail tip — a very slight upward curl held static.
- **Body:** Slight lean toward the raised paw side.
- **Motion:** Very slow, reduced breathing. Almost completely still — all energy is directed inward.

---

### Excited

> *"Oh! Right now!"*

- **Eyes:** Wide open — maximum size (115% of resting). Both highlights are clearly visible.
- **Mouth:** Open wide in a smile. The warmest, most open expression in Mitra's vocabulary.
- **Body:** A small body bounce — the squash-and-stretch bounce animation, as defined in [Animation Principles](#4-animation-principles).
- **Ears:** Both ears angled forward and slightly inward — fully attentive.
- **Paws:** Slightly raised, open — mirroring the open mouth. Mitra may appear to have both paws off the ground for one frame of the bounce.
- **Tail:** A wag — slightly faster than happy, but still not frantic. Maximum 1.5 cycles per second.
- **Head:** No tilt — direct and forward.

**Critical constraint:** Excited is still *Mitra's* excited. It must remain soft. The maximum amplitude is lower than you think. If it looks like a cartoon character getting startled, dial it back by 50%.

## 6. Design Rules — What Mitra Never Does

These are absolute. No exceptions. No edge cases.

### Expression rules

**Never angry.** Anger is incompatible with Mitra's character. There is no furrowed brow, no glare, no clenched paw. If a user tries to trigger an "angry Mitra" through any means, Mitra expresses concern instead.

**Never aggressive.** Mitra does not lunge, does not point, does not have any posture that reads as assertive or dominant.

**Never exaggerated cartoon reactions.** No limbs flailing. No flying off screen. No dramatic sweat drops or anime-style tension marks. Mitra's emotional range is real — not theatrical.

**Never sad.** Sadness implies something is wrong with Mitra's world. Mitra's world is always fundamentally okay. Concern is permitted. Sadness is not. The distinction is: concern faces outward (toward the user), sadness faces inward (toward Mitra). Mitra does not make situations about itself.

**Never embarrassed.** No blush in negative contexts. The optional blush exists for *happy* states only.

**Never victimized.** Mitra does not cower, shrink, or shield itself from the user. It is always safe.

### Motion rules

**Never move fast.** The maximum speed of any animation is deliberate and slow. If an animation completes in under 300ms, it is probably too fast.

**Never cut.** There are no instant transitions. Every state change is a transition, and every transition has an easing curve.

**Never loop with a visible seam.** All looping animations (idle breathing, tail sway) must have smooth loop points. The animation must be indistinguishable from continuous natural motion.

**Never draw attention to itself while the user is working.** Mitra does not initiate visible animations while the user is in an active typing session. Mitra becomes quieter — not more active — during focus states.

**Never flicker.** No frame-rate issues, no stuttering, no visual instability. Mitra's presence should be as calming as a steadily burning candle.

### Visual rules

**Never use pure black.** The darkest value in Mitra's design is `#2C1508`. Pure black makes Mitra feel heavy and cold.

**Never use gradients that fight the form.** Shading follows the implied light source. The light source is always soft, ambient, slightly above and to the right.

**Never change the proportions.** The head/body ratio is fixed. Do not scale the head down to make Mitra look "more mature." Do not inflate the body to make Mitra look "bigger."

**Never add text or UI to Mitra's body.** No notification badges. No progress rings. No system status overlays placed on the character sprite itself.

**Never use Mitra's likeness outside the companion window without design review.** This includes icons, splash screens, and marketing materials that deviate from the canonical art.

## 7. Accessibility

Mitra's design must remain fully communicative under visual constraints that real users experience daily.

### Minimum legible size: 64×64 pixels

At 64×64, the following elements **must** survive:

| Element | At 64×64 |
|---|---|
| Head/body distinction | Preserved via proportion difference |
| Eye highlight dots | Rendered as single pixels — must be present |
| Eye patch / dark regions | Must contrast clearly against orange fur |
| Ear silhouette | Both pointed ears visible in outline |
| Tail arc | Tail must read as a distinct curved form |
| Cream face mask | Must be distinguishable from orange body |

At 64×64, the following elements **may be simplified:**

| Element | Simplification permitted |
|---|---|
| Tail ring stripes | May reduce to 2 cream rings instead of 4 |
| Inner ear fill | May be omitted |
| Chest tuft detail | May flatten to solid cream region |
| Nose detail | May reduce to a single dark pixel or small dark oval |

### Color contrast

The dark eye patches (`#2C1508`) against the cream face mask (`#F2E0C0`) must achieve a minimum contrast ratio of **4.5:1** in all rendering contexts. This combination naturally achieves approximately 9:1 — do not compromise it.

The overall orange body must remain distinguishable from its shadow areas. Do not flatten the shading in ways that make Mitra appear as a flat orange silhouette.

### Motion accessibility

All of Mitra's animations must be suppressible. The host application must provide a mechanism to reduce or disable motion, responding to the OS-level `prefers-reduced-motion` setting.

When motion is reduced:

- All looping animations (breathing, tail sway, gaze drift) stop
- Mitra holds its last expression frame in a static pose
- State transitions become instant cross-fades (200ms opacity) instead of position-based animations
- The blink animation is retained at reduced frequency (one blink every 10 seconds) — it is a core life signal

### Color independence

Mitra's emotional states must be distinguishable by shape alone. A user with full deuteranopia or protanopia must be able to read the difference between Mitra's alert and sleepy states purely from eye shape and posture.

Test all emotional states by viewing in grayscale. If a state is only distinguishable by color, redesign it.

### Focus and interaction regions

The interactive region of the companion window must extend slightly beyond Mitra's visible silhouette — approximately 8px in all directions. Small targets are inaccessible targets.

## 8. Animation Timing Reference

All timings are in milliseconds. All easing curves are given in CSS cubic-bezier notation.

### Core easing curves

| Name | Curve | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Natural settle | `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.2, 0.64, 1)` | Head tilts, ear movements — gentle overshoot spring |
| Organic ease | `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)` | Body sways, breathing — smooth and symmetrical |
| Soft out | `cubic-bezier(0.0, 0.0, 0.2, 1)` | Settling into a resting state — decelerates gently |
| Soft in | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 1.0, 1)` | Departing from a resting state — accelerates gradually |

### Transition timings

| Transition | Duration | Easing |
|---|---|---|
| Emotional state change | 500ms | Organic ease |
| Head tilt — engage | 450ms | Natural settle |
| Head tilt — return | 300ms | Soft out |
| Eye size change (curious, excited) | 200ms | Soft out |
| Eye close (blink) | 40ms | Soft in |
| Eye open (blink) | 80ms | Soft out |
| Body bounce cycle | 600ms | `ease-in-out` |
| Walk step cycle | 400ms per step | Organic ease |
| Yawn animation (full) | 800ms | Soft in → Soft out |
| Idle → sitting transition | 1200ms | Soft out |
| Sitting → idle transition | 800ms | Natural settle |
| Appear (window open) | 600ms | Natural settle (from 80% scale) |
| Disappear (window close) | 400ms | Soft in (to 80% scale, 0 opacity) |

### Looping animation periods

| Animation | Period | Jitter |
|---|---|---|
| Breathing | 3500ms | ±300ms |
| Body micro-sway | 7000ms | ±1500ms |
| Tail drift (idle) | 6000ms | ±1000ms |
| Gaze drift | 16000ms | ±4000ms |
| Blink interval | 4500ms | ±2000ms |
| Happy tail wag | 1000ms | ±100ms |
| Excited tail wag | 666ms | ±50ms |
